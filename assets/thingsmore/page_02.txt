ThingsMore
Inspired by the northern Chinese term "事儿" (matter) and the southern Chinese term "士多店" (store)
Initiated by artists, designers, and individuals with diverse identities
Dedicated to independent thinking and forming unique creations
Responding to everyday life
Without shying away from the roughness and specifics of the era,
it preserves an inner order and a spiritual soil for the uniqueness of the individual.
